# HubBLE

HubBLE is a tiny ESP32-S3 USB dongle that connects up to four Bluetooth Low Energy keyboards and mice at once and
presents them to the host PC as one standard wired USB HID device. The host needs no Bluetooth, drivers or software: it
works in the BIOS, at login screens and on locked corporate PCs. A built-in web app, served over the dongle's own Wi-Fi
access point, handles pairing, settings, diagnostics and over-the-air updates.
